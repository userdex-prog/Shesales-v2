Every SheSales creation is handmade with love and care. Please read our policy carefully before placing an order.

All our products are handmade with love and care, so we do not accept returns or exchanges once an order has been delivered.

Wrong or Damaged Products

If you receive a wrong or different product, or your product arrives damaged, please contact SheSales within 24 hours of delivery.

To help us review the issue, provide clear photos or videos of the package and the product you received.

How We Review a Complaint

Once you contact us, SheSales will review the details and the supporting photos or videos you provide.

If the issue is reviewed and approved, we will provide a suitable replacement or another appropriate resolution.

A replacement or resolution is subject to review and approval by SheSales.

How to Report an Issue

  1. Contact SheSales within 24 hours of delivery.
  2. Explain whether the product is incorrect or arrived damaged.
  3. Provide clear photos or videos of the package and product.
  4. Wait for SheSales to review the information and respond with the next steps.

Please keep your order details available when contacting us so we can identify the purchase.

Refund Information

SheSales reviews reports involving incorrect or damaged products and, if approved, provides a suitable replacement or resolution.

A monetary refund is not guaranteed under this policy. Any refund or alternative resolution must be confirmed by SheSales after reviewing the reported issue and applicable requirements.

Important Information

Please inspect your order after delivery and report any incorrect or damaged product within the specified 24-hour period.

Reports made after this period may not meet the stated reporting requirement. Contact SheSales if you need clarification about a specific situation.

This policy reflects the return and damage-reporting terms provided by SheSales. Any additional refund rights required by applicable consumer law remain unaffected.
